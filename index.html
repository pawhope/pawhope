ABOUT PAWHOPE

A Small Act of Kindness Can Change a Life.

PawHope exists to support vulnerable animals who have been abandoned, injured or left without proper care.

Every contribution helps provide food, medical treatment, emergency veterinary care and a safer future for animals in need.

We believe that no animal should have to face suffering alone.

🐾

Every Life Matters

Together, we can give vulnerable animals the care and compassion they deserve.

MAKE A DIFFERENCE

❤️ How Your Donation Helps 🐾

£5

Provides nutritious meals for several hungry street cats or dogs.

£10

Supplies food and fresh water for multiple animals for several days.

£20

Helps pay for essential medication, flea and parasite treatment, or wound care.

£35

Covers a veterinary examination and basic treatment for a sick or injured animal.

£50

Helps fund emergency veterinary care for an animal in urgent need.

£100+

Supports larger rescue efforts, surgeries, long-term recovery and ongoing care for vulnerable animals.

100% of all donations go towards our cause. Every donation, regardless of its size, brings us one step closer to giving these animals the safety, care and compassion they deserve. Thank you for helping us save lives. ❤️
