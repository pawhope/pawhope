Why We Exist

Every day, thousands of street cats and dogs across Turkey face hunger, illness, injury, and abandonment with no one to care for them. They are left to survive on their own, often without food, shelter, or access to the medical treatment they desperately need. We exist because we believe every animal deserves kindness, compassion, and the chance to live free from unnecessary suffering. Through the support of generous people like you, we can provide food, veterinary care, and hope to the animals who need it most. Together, we can give them the brighter future they deserve.

🐾 How Your Donation Helps 🐾

  • £5 – Provides nutritious meals for several hungry street cats or dogs.
  • £10 – Supplies food and fresh water for multiple animals for several days.
  • £20 – Helps pay for essential medication, flea and parasite treatment, or wound care.
  • £35 – Covers a veterinary examination and basic treatment for a sick or injured animal.
  • £50 – Helps fund emergency veterinary care for an animal in urgent need.
  • £100+ – Supports larger rescue efforts, surgeries, long-term recovery, and ongoing care for vulnerable animals. *100% of all donations go towards our cause. Every donation, regardless of its size, brings us one step closer to giving these animals the safety, care, and compassion they deserve. Thank you for helping us save lives.
